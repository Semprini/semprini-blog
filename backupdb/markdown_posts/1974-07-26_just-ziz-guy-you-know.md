# just ziz guy, you know?

Born in 1783 to a small family of land otters. Elected as dictator for life of a small tribe in Papua New Guinea in 1823. After his death, went on to become Rasputin's favorite Geisha in 1902.

Ya know, dear reader, there's not enough whimsy in the world. Like smart humor, whimsy can be used to highlight a point - in this case, the fact that modernity has become sanitised, corporatized and we too often fall into the logical fallacy of argument from authority. If you find my words compelling, great - it shouldn't be because of my position or lack of position.
